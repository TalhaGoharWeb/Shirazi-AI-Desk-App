## Brand & Style
This design system marries the timeless dignity of classical Islamic scholarship with the precision of cutting-edge artificial intelligence. Tailored for researchers, jurists, students of knowledge, and seekers of nuanced legal discourse, it treats digital interfaces as digital manuscripts—places of quiet authority, intellectual gravity, and reverence.

The aesthetic fuses **Glassmorphic Depth** with a **Digital Heritage Luxury** feel. It prioritizes dark, focused environments reminiscent of rare-book archives at night, highlighted by luminous botanical inks, gold leafing, and fine structural filaments. Interfaces feel deliberate, measured, and free of superficial noise.

Key emotional tenets:
- **Intellectual Sanctuary:** Uncluttered layouts and restrained luminosity cultivate deep focus and meditative study.
- **Sovereign Authority:** Deep emeralds, obsidian fields, and antique gold call back to traditional gilded leather bindings and illuminated codices without appearing archaic.
- **Precision AI:** Razor-thin micro-borders, translucent frosted tiers, and clean mathematical geometries present computational fatwa indexing and comparative jurisprudence with modern clarity.
- **Native Bidirectionality:** Designed inherently around right-to-left (RTL) primacy for sacred scripts alongside first-class left-to-right (LTR) comparative scholastic engines.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in an ultra-deep obsidian slate foundation illuminated by antique metals and gemstone accents.

- **Primary (`#D4AF37` Antique Gold):** Represents historical authority, canonical citations, verified rulings, and primary action points. Used for active scholarly badges, illuminated verse markers, and principal focus anchors.
- **Secondary (`#10B981` Radiant Jade):** Symbolizes living legal consensus, affirmative validations, high-confidence AI semantic matches, and positive navigation states.
- **Tertiary (`#E2C974` Muted Gold):** Used for auxiliary highlights, commentary tags, hover accents, and secondary text adornments.
- **Neutral Palette:**
  - **Obsidian Base (`#0A0F0D`):** Absolute canvas ground, absorbing visual glare to mimic a moonlit manuscript desk.
  - **Deep Emerald Base (`#0D1F1A`):** Ambient background gradient undertone and high-level surface layer.
  - **Frosted Dark Slate (`#142420`):** Floating reading panes, citation trays, modal cards, and input panels, framed by subtle borders (`#1F3830`).
  - **Text & Content Tone (`#F9FAF8` Crisp Ivory):** Primary text color for Arabic calligraphy and Latin body copy to ensure effortless, fatigue-free contrast.
  - **Meta Tone (`#94A3B8` Muted Sage/Slate):** Scholarly footnotes, metadata, isnad chains, and timestamp registers.

## Typography
Typographic rules reflect centuries of scholastic typesetting adapted for high-density multi-pane digital readouts.

- **Scholastic Display & Sacred Passages:** Handled via classical serif forms (`Noto Serif` / Amiri / Scheherazade New equivalents in production). Sacred texts and classical citations demand generous vertical leading (`lineHeight: 180%`) to support complex diacritical marks (tashkeel) without line-collision.
- **Analytical & Structural Text:** `Inter` handles operational surfaces, AI confidence metrics, side-by-side madhhab comparison trees, and translation glossaries with strict neutral legibility.
- **Bidirectional Alignment:** 
  - RTL scripts trigger automatic right-alignment, with punctuation flipped and baseline adjustments applied to prevent diacritic clipping.
  - Dual-pane configurations (e.g., Arabic Matn on the right, English Scholarly Commentary on the left) feature synchronized baseline alignment across columns.
- **Numerals:** Tabular figures are used for verse numbers, volume-page indices, and hijri dates to ensure vertical stability in canonical lists.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout leverages a **Fluid Scholastic Grid** designed to switch effortlessly between single-scroll study and multi-column comparative analysis.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid structure with a maximum content canvas of 1440px. Supports a 3-column split: Left Navigator/Index (2.5 cols), Central Manuscript & AI Query Workspace (6 cols), and Right Scholarly Footnotes/Chain Analysis (3.5 cols).
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with collapsible metadata rails and persistent bottom query tray.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column compact layout. Reading panes become full-width with a stacked drawer interface for scholarly commentary and fatwa reference chains.
- **Directional Adaptation:** Gutter and margin indices flip automatically in RTL environments. Margins retain generous breathing room around theological texts to reflect traditional wide margins used by scholars for marginalia (hashiyah).

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is constructed through atmospheric illumination and selective transparency rather than standard heavy drop shadows.

- **The Manuscript Depth Stack:**
  - **Ground Plane (Base):** Solid `#0A0F0D` accented by radial ambient glows of `#0D1F1A` positioned behind active inquiry cards.
  - **Tier 1 (Surface Containers):** Background color `#142420` rendered at 80% opacity with a `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and a subtle `1px` outer border in `#1F3830`.
  - **Tier 2 (Interactive Floating Modules & Modals):** Semi-opaque `#182C27` (85% opacity) accompanied by an ultra-soft ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 0 0 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
  - **Tier 3 (Popovers & Contextual Lexicons):** High-blur frosted glass with an internal top hairline glow: `inset 0 1px 1px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)`.
- **Illuminated Accents:** Active focus nodes emit a soft, localized gold halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`), conveying an illuminated manuscript effect without harsh glare.

## Shapes
Interfaces balance historical geometric arches with architectural softness. 

- Outer cards, scholarly panels, and central input viewports feature unified **16px to 20px radii** (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`), creating comfortable, organic frame boundaries that soften the dense text.
- Micro-elements such as citation pills, status markers, and madhhab tags use controlled soft geometry to maintain an orderly tabular rhythm.
- Structural borders are strictly 1px micro-lines (`#1F3830`), keeping panels sharp and defined against dark, atmospheric backdrops.

## Components

### Buttons & Actions
- **Primary Scholarly Action:** Filled with Antique Gold (`#D4AF37`), displaying crisp dark obsidian text (`#0A0F0D`) with medium weight. Subtle inner glow along the top border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`).
- **Secondary / Analysis Trigger:** Frosted emerald slate surface (`#142420`) with a 1px border (`#1F3830`) and crisp ivory text (`#F9FAF8`). Hover triggers an amber border transition to `#D4AF37` at 50% opacity.
- **Ghost Actions:** Transparent with Muted Gold (`#E2C974`) text; active state displays an ambient jade background highlight (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`).

### Input Fields & Prompt Consoles
- **Deep Research Input:** Elevated frosted dark container (`#142420`, 85% opacity, blur 16px) with an edge ring in `#1F3830`.
- **Focus State:** Border shifts to `#D4AF37` with an inner radiant glow (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)`). Placeholder text rests in Muted Sage (`#94A3B8`). Supports multi-line input with automatic RTL/LTR detection based on the first character entered.

### Cards & Reading Panels
- **Fatwa Dossier Card:** Framed in 16px rounded borders with frosted slate fill. Features an ornamental micro-bar on the leading edge (Gold for canonical fatwas, Jade for consensus verdicts).
- **Scripture Matn Block:** Distinctive surface treatment featuring increased line spacing, ivory calligraphy, and an optional subtle arabesque watermark at 3% opacity in the background.

### Chips & Badges
- **Jurisprudential School (Madhhab) Tags:** Pill-shaped, semi-transparent dark emerald badges with 1px border. Text in `#E2C974` with an optional leading micro-dot representing scholarly consensus levels.
- **Verification Badges:** Soft Jade background (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`) paired with `#10B981` text and micro-icons indicating authenticated isnad (chain of transmission).

### Lists & Chain-of-Transmission Trees
- **Isnad Timeline / Scholarly Lineage:** Vertical timeline running on a 1px antique gold thread (`#D4AF37` at 30% opacity). Nodes feature 8px circular jade emblems with hoverable scholar biographical previews.
- **Comparative Jurisprudence Table:** Alternating frosted rows with sticky header markers and subtle column dividers in `#1F3830`.
